Product summary

Zone: App Blocker & Focus is an iPhone and iPad productivity app by independent developer Vikas Saini. It combines timed focus sessions with selected app and website restrictions, optional Strict Mode, Live Activities, Dynamic Island support, and private focus insights.

What makes Zone distinct

  • The focus timer and blocker are one workflow.
  • Strict Mode adds commitment for sessions that should not be easy to abandon.
  • Blocking selections are opaque to the app and developer.
  • Live Activities keep the countdown visible without reopening the app.
