the durable source of truth for the project's purpose, positioning,
constraints, analytics strategy, success criteria, and prohibited tactics.

Keep responses to the owner concise unless a detailed deliverable is explicitly
requested.

- Lead with strict app and website blocking; use privacy as supporting proof.
- Keep all product, pricing, platform, data-flow, and privacy claims factual.
- Build static, fast, accessible, crawlable pages suitable for GitHub Pages.
- Treat SEO and AI discovery as an evidence-based long-term strategy.
- Do not add paid services, tracking systems, or third-party dependencies
  without explicit approval.
- Do not use fake reviews, fabricated ratings, bought backlinks, keyword
  stuffing, or mass-generated thin content.
- Validate pages, metadata, structured data, links, responsive behavior, and
  accessibility before publishing.
